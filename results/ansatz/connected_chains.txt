   Two fits are reported and they are NOT the same thing:
     ORACLE   coefficients least-squares fitted to lambda_3^exact. This is an UPPER BOUND
              on what the span can reach, not a closure -- it has seen the answer.
     CLOSURE  coefficients determined ONLY by requiring Tr_3 lambda_3 = anti(E, lambda_2),
              which never looks at lambda_3. This IS a closure, with no free parameters.
   If the ORACLE coefficients agree across systems and states, the span is universal; if
