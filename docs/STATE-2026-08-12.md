## The reframing

TDHF is stable because it is the exact dynamics of a state family (determinants), equivalently a
commutator flow on the closed algebra `u(M)`; cumulant truncations `λ_k = 0` for `k ≥ 3` are not states
(Marcinkiewicz-type), so no algebraic dressing makes them behave like states. The Bravyi–Kitaev /
Jozsa–Miyake universality theorem is the precise reason no polynomial Lie algebra containing all one-body
operators and any interaction exists. Positivity must therefore come from the state, the representation
(XR), the generator (CP), or projection — not from truncation.
